Na egzaminie praca to zwykle opis zawodu kogoś z rodziny albo plany na przyszłość.

**Opis zawodu.** _My aunt is a vet. She works full-time in a small clinic and looks after sick animals. She earns a good salary, but she often works at weekends._

**Plany.** _In the future I'd like to work as a programmer, because I'm good at maths and I like computers._

**Wakacyjna praca.** _Last summer I got a part-time job in a café. I had to serve customers and clean the tables._
