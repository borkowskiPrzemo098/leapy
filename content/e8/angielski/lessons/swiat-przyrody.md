Przyroda pojawia się na egzaminie w dwóch rolach: opis pogody i miejsca oraz problemy środowiska.

**Pogoda i miejsce.** _We spent a week in the mountains. The weather was terrible on the first day, with heavy rain and strong wind, but then it was sunny._

**Ochrona środowiska.** _Air pollution is a big problem in many cities. We can help if we use public transport, recycle plastic and save energy at home._

**Zwierzęta.** _Pandas are an endangered species, so they are protected in special reserves._
