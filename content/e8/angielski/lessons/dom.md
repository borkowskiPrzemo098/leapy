W opisie domu porządek pomaga: najpierw gdzie, potem jaki, na końcu co w nim robisz.

**Gdzie i jaki.** _We live in a flat on the third floor. It isn't very big, but there is a balcony with a view of the park._

**Pomieszczenia i meble.** _In my bedroom there is a wardrobe, a desk and a bed by the window._ Kuchnia ma _a sink_, _a fridge_ i _a cooker_.

**Obowiązki.** _I usually do the washing-up after dinner and my brother takes out the rubbish._ W takich zdaniach często pojawiają się _usually_, _always_, _on Saturdays_: to Present Simple.
