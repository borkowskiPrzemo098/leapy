Słownictwo z kultury przydaje się najbardziej, gdy trzeba coś polecić albo ocenić.

**Opowiadanie o wyjściu.** _Last weekend we went to the theatre to see a play about two brothers. The actors were brilliant and the audience gave them a standing ovation._

**Ocena.** Unikaj samego _good_ i _bad_. Lepiej: _The special effects were amazing, but the plot was a bit predictable._

**Polecenie.** _If you like comedies, you should definitely see it. It's worth watching._ Po _worth_ stoi czasownik z _-ing_.
