W tekstach o sporcie najczęściej opisujemy mecz albo swoje treningi.

**Mecz.** _On Saturday our team played against the best team in town. We scored in the last minute and won 2–1. The fans went crazy!_

**Treningi.** _I go swimming twice a week and I play volleyball at school. Our coach says I'm getting better._

**Kontuzja.** _Unfortunately, I hurt my knee during the match. It's not a serious injury, but I can't play for two weeks._
